About

Operational leadership with financial discipline

Timothy Nesmith is the founder of PhillyScreen LLC and a cross-functional operations leader whose work connects project management, finance, field execution, vendors, and stakeholders. He has supported public engagement programs, venue financial operations, merchandise production, e-commerce, entertainment projects, and partnership development.

His approach is practical: define the work, assign ownership, make cost and schedule visible, report exceptions early, and close with evidence. Portfolio demonstrations are clearly labeled, and real outcomes are limited to documented experience.
